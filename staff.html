Weston College · Digital Inclusion

Learning Without Barriers

How we create the conditions for every learner to participate fully in digital learning, regardless of ability, language, background, connectivity, or prior experience.

Every learner and member of staff at Weston College can access, engage with, and benefit from digital learning, regardless of ability, language, background, connectivity, or prior experience. That is the standard we are working towards.

QFT alignment

Quality First Teaching connection. When a staff member runs the Accessibility Checker before uploading a resource, they are doing QFT. When they enable Live Captions as default in every Teams session, they are doing QFT. When they show Immersive Reader to every learner at induction rather than only those with a recorded need, they are doing QFT. Learning Without Barriers is a practical QFT framework for the digital age.

Five principles

Principle 1

Inclusion by design, not by reaction

Built in from the start — curriculum design, IT procurement, resources. WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor, not the ceiling.

Principle 2

Start with our most excluded learners

ESOL and SEND learners face the sharpest digital barriers. Design for them and it works for everyone.

Principle 3

Everyone is responsible

Not one team or role. Teaching staff, IT, student services, and leadership — whole college.

Principle 4

Quick wins build momentum

Immersive Reader, Live Captions, Accessibility Checker, Translator. Free. Available now. No budget needed.

Principle 5

Learner voice is non-negotiable

Learners are genuine co-designers, not a consultation tick-box. Their experience must drive decisions.

Three pillars

Foundations

WCAG 2.2 AA for all uploaded materials. Immersive Reader, Live Captions, and the Accessibility Checker embedded in everyday practice.

Systems and culture

Consistent Teams standards. Separate CPD pathways for staff and learner digital skills. A Digital Resource Quality Standard for every upload.

Equity and empowerment

Genuine learner voice. Good Things Foundation partnership. A Student Digital Inclusion Advisory Group with real influence.
